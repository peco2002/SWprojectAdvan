KeyPoints
(cid:2) Evidenceontheassociationbetweenrunningdistance,duration,frequency,orintensityorrecentchangesinthese
parameters and the onset of lower limb running-related injuries is conflicting.
(cid:2) No universal recommendations on training parameters or progressions can be issued based on the current
evidence; the popular ‘‘10% rule’’ for increasing weekly distance is not justified.
(cid:2) The lack of reporting guidelines for running-related injuries contributes to high heterogeneity in the definition of
injury, runner profiles, and follow-up periods.
